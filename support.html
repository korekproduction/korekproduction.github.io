Frequently asked questions

  • I didn't receive my SMS code. Wait for the resend timer, check your signal, and confirm the number is correct. Some regions restrict SMS delivery; if it persists, email us.
  • I was signed out on my other phone. PulseCalls allows one device per phone number; signing in on a new device signs out the old one.
  • How do I delete my account and data? Email us from a message that includes your sign-up phone number and we will delete your account and associated data as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Calls have no sound or poor quality. Check microphone permission in system settings and try switching between speaker and earpiece in the call screen.
